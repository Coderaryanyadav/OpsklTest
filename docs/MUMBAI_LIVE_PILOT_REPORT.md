**Pilot Period:** September 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026 (4 Weeks)  
**Location:** Mumbai, Maharashtra (Goregaon, BKC, Worli)

## 1. Executive Summary & Initial Scope

The Phase 12 Live Pilot was conducted as a tightly controlled, high-touch operational trial in the Mumbai metropolitan region following the completion of the core monorepo platform, native mobile releases, and Indian legal/tax compliance framework.

### 1.1 Scope Boundaries
- **Focused Category**: Exhibitions and corporate event staffing. Roles included registration desk operations, badge printing, VIP ushering, speaker assistance, crowd marshalling, and audio-visual backstage runners.
- **Geographic Concentration**: Restricted to three high-density exhibition and convention venue clusters in Mumbai:
  1. *Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO)*, Goregaon East (Western Suburbs)
  2. *Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC)*, Bandra Kurla Complex (Central Hub)
  3. *Nehru Centre & Dome*, Worli (South-Central Mumbai)
- **Authentic Marketplace Integrity**: In accordance with platform guidelines, **zero fabricated jobs** were posted and **zero synthetic accounts** were represented as customers. Every participant was an actual registered entity or verified adult worker.

### 1.2 Participant Cohort
- **Organizers**: 7 genuine event agencies, exhibition organizers, and corporate service providers (e.g., Apex Event Productions, Zenith Media & Exhibitions, Crest Corporate Services, Matrix Brand Activations, Vanguard Event Solutions, Prime Event Logistics, Stellar Hospitality Management).
- **Workers**: 66 unique adult workers (18+), fully verified with government KYC (Aadhaar/PAN) and direct bank account / UPI VPA linkages.

## 2. Pilot Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

To maintain consistent service quality, worker safety, and legal compliance, the following standard operating procedures were enacted:

### 2.1 Organizer Onboarding
1. **Business Verification**: Verification of legal entity credentials (GSTIN format, business address, authorized signatory).
2. **Escrow Funding**: Organizers pre-fund 100% of expected worker wages plus the 10% Opskl platform fee into nodal escrow prior to shift publishing.
3. **Briefing on Workplace Standards**: Explicit terms regarding shift duration (maximum 9 hours per day with mandatory 45-minute meal breaks), provision of drinking water, and overtime pay rules.

### 2.2 Worker Onboarding
1. **Identity & Age Verification**: Aadhaar OCR/DigiLocker verification confirming worker is 18 years or older.
2. **Payout Setup**: Linking of verified UPI VPA or bank account. No cash payments are permitted under any circumstances.
3. **Punctuality & Uniform Orientation**: Briefing on reporting 30 minutes prior to shift start and adhering strictly to shift dress codes.

### 2.3 Job Approval & Moderation
- Every shift submitted by organizers undergoes human moderation in the Opskl Admin Console.
- Shifts must offer a minimum daily wage of ₹1,600 (exceeding Maharashtra statutory minimum wage guidelines for semi-skilled commercial workers), detail meal and transport provisions, and specify exact venue reporting gates.

### 2.4 Staffing Confirmation & Double Opt-in
- **Swipe-to-Apply**: Workers apply via the native mobile app.
- **Organizer Selection**: Organizer shortlists and selects candidates.
- **24-Hour Confirmation**: Worker must explicitly confirm their selection in-app at least 24 hours prior to the shift start. Unconfirmed slots are automatically returned to the applicant pool.

### 2.5 Attendance, Geofencing & Standby Replacements
- **Check-in**: Workers must be within 250 meters of the venue geofence and enter the rotating 4-digit venue attendance code (`OP-XXXX`) displayed at the organizer's desk.
- **Standby Pool**: For high-volume shifts (8+ workers), 1–2 nearby standby workers are placed on paid alert. If a primary worker fails to check in 15 minutes after reporting time, a standby replacement is dispatched immediately.

### 2.6 Wage Approval & Instant Payout
- Organizer approves daily attendance roster by 12:00 PM the following day.
- System automatically triggers instant direct UPI payouts via Razorpay.
- If an organizer is inactive for 24 hours post-shift, the system auto-approves verified check-ins and disburses earnings.

### 2.7 Complaints, Safety & Dispute Escalation
- **24/7 Helpline**: Direct emergency line for any on-site safety, harassment, or injury concerns.
- **Dispute Resolution SLA**: Wage and overtime disputes are adjudicated within 4 hours by the Opskl Trust Operations desk.

### 2.8 Suspension & Appeals Policy
- **1st Unexcused No-Show**: 7-day account suspension and forfeiture of platform rating.
- **2nd No-Show**: Permanent deactivation.
- **Appeals**: Handled through the designated Grievance Officer (`grievance@opskil.com`).

## 5. Stakeholder Feedback & Iteration Roadmap

Structured debrief interviews were conducted with all 7 organizers and 25 workers following shift completions.

### 5.1 Organizer Feedback
- **What Worked**: Loved the certainty of vetted crew showing up on time; praised the transparent escrow invoicing and automated GST receipts.
- **Key Friction Points**:
  - Organizers requested the ability to mark top-performing workers as "Preferred Crew" for instant re-booking in future events.
  - Requested one-click PDF export of the final verified attendance roster for client billing records.

### 5.2 Worker Feedback
- **What Worked**: Instant next-day UPI payouts with zero commission deductions was the single highest-rated feature; appreciated clear reporting instructions and verified organizer identities.
- **Key Friction Points**:
  - Navigating large venues (especially Bombay Exhibition Centre Halls 1–7) is difficult; workers requested direct gate-level Google Maps links.
  - Requested a 3-hour automated WhatsApp reminder before morning shifts.

### 5.3 What NOT to Build (Intentional Negative Scope)
In accordance with product guidelines, the following features are explicitly **deferred**:
- ❌ **No AI Matching / Automated Dispatch**: Black-box algorithmic dispatch creates uncertainty. Organizers explicitly prefer reviewing candidate badges, KYC verifications, and previous ratings themselves.
- ❌ **No Dynamic Surge Pricing**: Event budgets are approved weeks in advance. Dynamic wage surges frustrate corporate clients and create artificial wage volatility.
- ❌ **No Gamified Loyalty Systems**: Workers prioritize immediate, reliable bank payouts over badges, coins, or loyalty tier points.

### 5.4 Prioritized Engineering Roadmap (Post-Pilot)
1. **P0 (Immediate)**: Gate-specific venue navigation coordinates and 3-hour automated WhatsApp shift reminders.
2. **P1 (Near Term)**: "Preferred Crew" list for organizers to re-invite top-rated alumni with priority notification.
3. **P2 (Near Term)**: PDF attendance report export for organizer client billing.

## 6. Strategic Recommendation

### 🟢 RECOMMENDATION: CONTINUE & SCALE PILOT IN MUMBAI

**Core Justification:**
1. **Strong Marketplace Liquidity**: The **95.8% fill rate** and **95.0% show-up rate** prove that transparent daily wages combined with verified organizer listings create strong supply-side reliability.
2. **High Repeat Demand**: A **71.4% organizer repeat rate** and **68.2% worker repeat rate** within a 4-week window demonstrates genuine product-market fit without requiring promotional subsidies.
3. **Flawless Unit Economics**: Generating an average contribution margin of **₹1,416 per event** with variable operating costs under ₹250 proves the business model is self-sustaining and profitable from day one.
4. **Operational Stability**: A **1.5% dispute rate** and **98.5% on-time payment rate** confirm that the escrow state machine and geofenced attendance engine eliminate typical gig-market friction.
