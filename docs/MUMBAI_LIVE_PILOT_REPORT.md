## 1. Executive Summary & Planned Rollout Scope

The Mumbai Rollout Operating Plan establishes a tightly controlled, high-touch operational framework for launching the Opskl monorepo platform, native mobile apps, and legal/tax compliance infrastructure in India's commercial capital.

### 1.1 Scope Boundaries
- **Focused Category**: Exhibitions and corporate event staffing. Roles include registration desk operations, badge printing, VIP ushering, speaker assistance, crowd marshalling, and audio-visual backstage runners.
- **Geographic Concentration**: Restricted to three high-density exhibition and convention venue clusters in Mumbai:
  1. *Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO)*, Goregaon East (Western Suburbs)
  2. *Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC)*, Bandra Kurla Complex (Central Hub)
  3. *Nehru Centre & Dome*, Worli (South-Central Mumbai)
- **Marketplace Integrity Standard**: Real-world operations strictly mandate **zero fabricated listings** and **zero synthetic accounts**. Every live participant must be a legally registered business entity or government-verified adult worker (18+).

### 1.2 Target Participant Cohort Model
- **Target Organizers**: 7 pilot partner agencies (event production houses, trade show organizers, and corporate agency partners).
- **Target Worker Pool**: 60–70 adult workers (18+), fully verified through government KYC (Aadhaar/PAN) and direct bank account / UPI VPA linkages.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

To maintain consistent service quality, worker safety, and statutory compliance, the following standard operating procedures govern the pilot:

### 2.1 Organizer Onboarding SOP
1. **Business Verification**: Verification of legal entity credentials (GSTIN format, registered address, authorized signatory identity).
2. **Escrow Funding**: Organizers pre-fund 100% of expected worker wages plus the 10% Opskl platform fee into nodal escrow prior to shift publishing.
3. **Workplace Standards Briefing**: Explicit contractual terms regarding shift duration (maximum 9 hours per day with mandatory 45-minute meal breaks), clean drinking water provision, and overtime pay rules.

### 2.2 Worker Onboarding SOP
1. **Identity & Age Verification**: Aadhaar OCR/DigiLocker verification confirming worker is 18 years or older with valid government proof.
2. **Payout Setup**: Direct linkage of verified UPI VPA or bank account. Cash payments are prohibited across all platform interactions.
3. **Punctuality & Uniform Orientation**: Mandatory briefing on reporting 30 minutes prior to shift start and adhering strictly to event dress codes.

### 2.3 Job Approval & Moderation
- Every shift submitted by organizers undergoes administrative verification in the Opskl Admin Console.
- Shifts must offer a minimum wage of ₹100/hr (exceeding Maharashtra statutory living wage guidelines for semi-skilled commercial workers), detail meal and transport provisions, and specify exact venue reporting instructions and coordinates.

### 2.4 Staffing Confirmation & Double Opt-in
- **Swipe-to-Apply**: Workers apply via the native mobile app.
- **Organizer Selection**: Organizer shortlists and selects candidates from the live applicant roster.
- **24-Hour Confirmation**: Worker must explicitly confirm their selection in-app at least 24 hours prior to the shift start. Unconfirmed slots are automatically returned to the applicant pool.

### 2.5 Attendance, Geofencing & Standby Replacements
- **Check-in**: Workers must be within 250 meters of the venue geofence and enter the rotating venue attendance code (`OP-XXXX`) displayed at the organizer's desk.
- **Standby Pool**: For high-volume shifts (8+ workers), 1–2 nearby standby workers are placed on paid alert. If a primary worker fails to check in 15 minutes after reporting time, a standby replacement is dispatched immediately.

### 2.6 Wage Approval & Instant Payout
- Organizer reviews and approves daily attendance roster by 12:00 PM the following day.
- Platform executes direct UPI payouts via banking gateway partner.
- If an organizer remains inactive for 24 hours post-shift, the system auto-approves verified check-ins and disburses earnings.

### 2.7 Complaints, Safety & Dispute Escalation
- **24/7 Helpline**: Direct emergency line for on-site safety, harassment, or injury concerns.
- **Dispute Resolution SLA**: Wage and overtime disputes are adjudicated within 4 hours by the Opskl Trust Operations desk.

### 2.8 Suspension & Appeals Policy
- **1st Unexcused No-Show**: 7-day account suspension and forfeiture of platform reliability score.
- **2nd No-Show**: Permanent deactivation.
- **Appeals**: Handled through the designated Grievance Officer (`grievance@opskil.com`).

## 4. Platform Safety, Telemetry & Emergency Controls

The operational plan incorporates active risk mitigation mechanisms:

1. **Marketplace Emergency Pause Killswitch**:
   - Integrated in `packages/shared/src/services/system_killswitch_service.ts` and the Admin Operations Console.
   - Enables immediate suspension of shift applications and bookings during severe weather warnings (e.g., Mumbai monsoon waterlogging alerts) or system maintenance.
   - Broadcasts real-time warning banners across Worker, Organizer, and Admin interfaces.
2. **Target Telemetry Thresholds**:
   - API Availability SLA: **>= 99.9%**
   - Database Latency (p95): **< 100 ms**
   - Payment Reconciliation Discrepancies: **0 (Strict Zero-Tolerance)**
